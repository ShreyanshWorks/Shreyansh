Risk Prediction Model for Hospital Readmission

Hospital readmission costs make for a significant amount of hospital inpatient care expenses. Diabetes is not just one of the world’s top 10 leading causes of mortality, but it is also the most costly chronic disease in the United States. Diabetes patients in the hospital have a greater risk of readmission than those who do not have diabetes. As a result, lowering readmission rates for diabetes patients has the potential to considerably cut medical costs. The goal of this research is to forecast the possibility of a diabetic patient being readmitted. The dataset was collected from the University of California, Irvine’s Center for Machine Learning and Intelligent Systems and comprises over 100,000 characteristics and 50 variables such as the number of operations, drugs, and time spent in hospital etc. In this Project I have identified which factors are the strong predictors for predicting the readmission of diabetic patients and build a classification model with decent accuracy for predicting readmission.
